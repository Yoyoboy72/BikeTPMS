Wheel configuration

Speed is calculated from measured wheel RPM and the tire's actual rolling circumference. A measured rollout is more accurate than nominal tire size.

Effective circumference: 2,199 mm (from diameter). To measure rollout, mark a point on the tire, roll exactly one revolution with rider weight on the bike, then measure the ground distance.

Pressure & temperature

The MS5837-30BA sends absolute mbar. Enter ambient atmospheric pressure to display tire gauge pressure. Don't zero a sensor that's already in a pressurized tire. Recheck the ambient reference after significant altitude or weather changes.

No ambient reference set. Gauge pressure unavailable.
